At work, Mist wireless · 2025 to now

Config Wizard

The brief
Recreate an access point's WLAN configuration in another Mist org or site.
What I built
A desktop tool that reads the configuration from a support bundle or live from the device, lets you review and complete each WLAN, then recreates it through the Mist REST API. It has a dry-run mode, an audit log and HTML reports, and ships as a single executable. Teams across the org adopted it.

B.Tech capstone · 2025

WeedYOLO-MS

The brief
A detection model accurate enough to be useful and small enough to run at the edge.
What I built
Hybrid architectures that fuse a Swin Transformer backbone with CBAM attention on YOLOv9, then an ablation across fusion strategies. The best configuration reached 0.855 precision and mAP@0.50 with 3.56M parameters and 15.6 GFLOPs.

Python, PyTorch, YOLOv9, Swin Transformer, CBAM

Repository not yet public

Research · IEEE ICCCNT 2025

Speech recognition for Kannada

The brief
Kannada is a low-resource language for speech recognition. How far can a small, carefully chosen dataset take a fine-tuned model?
What I did
Curated 465 speech samples across five domains and fine-tuned OpenAI's Whisper on them. The fine-tuned model reached a word error rate of 0.0958 and a character error rate of 0.0321.

Python, PyTorch, Whisper, Hugging Face

Published at IEEE ICCCNT 2025

About

Following one problem all the way down.

I started in data, cleaning spacecraft telemetry at ISRO, and moved into networking at Juniper. Most of my days now are spent following a single fault across a device's firmware, a Kafka stream and a cloud API until the three stories agree.

I like the parts of a system where layers meet and disagree, and I like building the small tools that make those meetings easier to read. Outside work I teach with Make A Difference, a non-profit, and I took part in the Amrita to Ritsumeikan University student exchange.
